# Google Form – Sports Fan & Brand Survey (free, ~3 minutes)

Target: 80-120 respondents aged 18-34 (classmates, hostel groups, family circles, social media). Add a consent line: "Anonymous, for an academic project."

Export responses to CSV (Responses > Link to Sheets > Download CSV), rename the columns as below, save as `data/survey_responses.csv`.

| Column name | Question | Type |
|---|---|---|
| age_group | Your age group | 18-21 / 22-25 / 26-34 / 35+ |
| gender | Gender (optional) | Female / Male / Other / Prefer not to say |
| city_tier | Where do you live? | Metro / Tier-2 / Tier-3 or rural |
| weekly_watch_hours | Hours per week you watch or follow sports | Number |
| health_drink_freq | How often do you buy health/energy drinks? | 1 (never) - 5 (very often) |
| interest_cricket ... interest_running | How much do you follow: Cricket (IPL) / Kabaddi (PKL) / Football (ISL) / Badminton / Hockey / Running-marathons | 1-5 each |
| intent_cricket ... intent_running | If a health-drink brand sponsored this sport, how likely are you to try it? | 1-5 each |

Optional extra questions (for qualitative points in the report): "Name one sponsor you remember from a sport you follow" (brand recall), "Which platform do you use most to follow sports?" (TV / YouTube / OTT app / Instagram).
