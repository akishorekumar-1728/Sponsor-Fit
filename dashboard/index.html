Case Understanding

Case background

India's sports-sponsorship industry reached about ₹16,633 crore in 2024, roughly seven times its 2008 size, but cricket alone takes about 81–89% of that money (GroupM ESP, 2025; WPP Media, 2025). Brands who cannot afford cricket, or don't want its clutter, are turning to kabaddi, football, badminton, hockey and distance running instead.

This project follows FitFuel, a hypothetical mid-size Indian health-drink brand. It targets 18–34 year-old urban and semi-urban consumers, has a fixed annual sponsorship budget of ₹10 crore, and wants both brand awareness and measurable trial and sales.

Central problem

Sponsorship is expensive, long-term and hard to reverse. Cricket offers unmatched reach at a price and clutter level a mid-size brand cannot easily justify; smaller sports are cheaper and closer to the target audience but reach far fewer people. Which sport gives FitFuel the best balance of reach, fit, cost and risk, and how sure can we be about that answer?

Objectives

  • Describe the market, audience and social context of six candidate sports.
  • Build a transparent, evidence-based scoring model (the Brand–Sport Fit Index).
  • Rank the sports with three methods and stress-test the ranking against changing priorities and data errors.
  • Propose a practical sponsorship strategy, budget split and KPIs, and state the model's limits.
Application of Course Concepts

How the syllabus applies to this case

Sponsorship & the match-up hypothesis
Cornwell, Weeks & Roy, 2005; Fleck & Quester, 2007		Sponsorship transfers a sport's meaning to the brand, and works best when sponsor and sport share a logical link. A health drink fits endurance running more naturally than a generic cricket sponsorship — this is the image congruence criterion in the model.
Social identity & fan behaviour
Tajfel & Turner, 1979		Fans identify with regional and language communities. PKL broadcasts in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada; football's fan base is concentrated in Kerala, Goa, West Bengal and the North-East. This feeds the audience fit and engagement criteria.
Commercialisation of sport
IPL media-rights and franchise economics		IPL's 2024–28 title deal (₹500 cr/yr) versus PKL's historic title deal (₹60 cr/yr) shows why cricket is expensive — this drives the cost efficiency criterion.
Entertainment & digital media marketingIPL 2025 reached 652M people digitally versus 537M on TV; sponsorship must be built as always-on content, not a one-off logo — reflected in the activation budget.
Prediction / decision models
Saaty 1980 (AHP), Hwang & Yoon 1981 (TOPSIS), Shannon 1948 (entropy)		Three scoring methods combine the brand's judgement with objective data patterns; Monte Carlo simulation then predicts how often each sport would still win if priorities or data were slightly wrong.

Findings & Proposed Solution

Major findings

  • Running is the best fit for FitFuel's stated priorities. It has the highest audience fit and image congruence, and wins the primary model in nearly every scenario tested.
  • Kabaddi is the most consistent second choice — it ranks second under all three scoring methods (weighted-sum, TOPSIS, entropy), even though those methods disagree on who comes first.
  • Cricket only wins if reach dominates the decision (roughly 38% weight or more — see Trade-offs). Its very low cost efficiency and heavy sponsor clutter make it a poor fit for a ₹10 crore budget.
  • The evidence is uneven. Only audience reach is built from measured data; the other six criteria are expert ratings backed by cited sources, so the ranking was stress-tested (see "How sure are we?") rather than taken at face value.

Proposed strategy: a two-anchor portfolio

Primary partnership in distance running (city marathons, 10K series, run clubs) and a secondary partnership in the Pro Kabaddi League at team/category level, with a ring-fenced budget for digital content, activation and measurement.

Splitting the budget

The two best-scoring sports share the money in proportion to their squared scores, after setting aside a share for content, activation and measurement. Treat it as a starting point for negotiation.

Activation plan & KPIs

RunningHydration/recovery stations at city runs, a run-club programme, free fitness-app challenges, mid-tier athlete ambassadors. Negotiate category exclusivity first — other nutrition/water brands already sponsor major marathons.
KabaddiRegional-language content (Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Hindi), YouTube highlight clips, in-match sampling, a free-to-play (no money) prediction quiz.
KPIsBrand recall (pre/post pulse survey), trial & redemption (unique codes), content engagement (platform analytics), sentiment (free NLP), sales uplift in activated regions vs control regions.

Review each season: shift toward cricket-style digital/OTT ad packages only if the brand's goal moves toward mass awareness (reach weight above ~38%); increase kabaddi if reach targets are missed.

Innovation & Practical Relevance

What makes this approach original

  • Transparent data pipeline — every indicator carries a source, year and quality grade; cleaning steps are logged; imputed values (like running's reach) are flagged rather than hidden.
  • Decision map & break-even test — instead of one static winner, the model shows exactly how much the brand's priorities would need to shift before the recommendation changes (see Trade-offs).
  • Two-layer uncertainty testing — Monte Carlo simulation on both the priority weights and the rating scores shows how far the answer can be trusted, not just what the answer is.
  • Free, no-install interactive dashboard — this single HTML file runs in any browser; a marketing manager can change priorities with sliders and see every chart update live, with zero setup cost.

Emerging technology used

  • OTT/YouTube-first distribution and digital reach data (IPL 2025 digital reach: 652M vs 537M TV).
  • AI-based sentiment analysis of fan comments (free Hugging Face models) as a proposed KPI.
  • Fitness-app and wearable integration for running-challenge activation.
  • Google Trends as a free, optional demand-growth signal (see fetch_trends.py in the project files).

Limitations (stated for transparency)

Six of seven criteria are expert ratings, not measured values. Running's reach is imputed because it is a participation sport with no comparable TV/OTT audience figure. Cost efficiency is a relative index, not real rate-card pricing, and the PKL title-price benchmark is from 2017. A real fan survey (template included in the project files) would sharpen the audience-fit and engagement scores.
